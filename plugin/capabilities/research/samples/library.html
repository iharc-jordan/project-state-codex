Method

No provincial subsidy funds AI training as its own stream

Mandate: Is there a provincial subsidy specific to AI training for small businesses?

FIXTURE. A closed-negative study: the question was answered, and the answer was no.

The answer

There is no provincial programme that funds AI training for small businesses as a subsidy of its own. Of the eleven provincial training programmes, none lists AI as a stream RES-F-020; the programme office confirms that AI tools training can qualify only under the general digital-skills topic RES-F-021; and a search of every provincial registry found no AI-specific subsidy RES-F-022.

What the evidence says

  • Eligible topics across the eleven programmes include digital skills, leadership and safety — not AI on its own RES-F-020
  • AI training may be funded as digital skills, so a small business can claim it — as one eligible topic among many RES-F-021
  • The absence is recorded as a finding, not inferred from silence: the registry search is on file RES-F-022

How sure we are

All three findings are primary and established; the evidence gate (60%) passes at 100%. No challenge was raised against them.

What we did not find

A programme-registry search for an AI stream returned nothing usable (a barren step, recorded). No federal programme was in scope.

Method

Read every provincial programme page; searched the programme registries; confirmed the absence with the programme office's FAQ.
